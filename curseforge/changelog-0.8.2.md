## GuildSentinel 0.8.2

- Guild achievements: singular for the first weekly goal; names that are too long use a smaller font instead of being cut off.
- Professions: old raw-material entries from earlier versions (e.g. Felweed as "Alchemy") are removed automatically.

## GuildSentinel 0.8.1

### Fixed / improved
- Settings > Features: three columns, all switches and the button fit into the window.
- Leadership > Probation: "Promote" only with at least 40 % online days since joining (at least 3); percentage in the list.
- Leadership > Rank rights: test mode shows the test guild's ranks.
- Overview: departures count the same as Leadership > Departures; shorter "players online" text; hidden tiles leave no gap.
- My week: singular ("1 elixir"), clear markers (green + ready, orange ! missing), resistances on their own line.
- Chronicle: no duplicate entries, reported departures included; Discord export starts at the top.
- Guild achievements: wider name column.
- Loot wishes: dungeon preselected for your level, visible hint without a dungeon.
- Questions: "Remove" asks first; shorter field, "Show solved" fits.
- Addons page: heading "Addons by GoldSentinel".
- Professions: raw materials (e.g. Felweed) are removed from the watch list.
- Test mode: "Guild milestone" adds new max-level members instead of changing test characters.

## GuildSentinel 0.8.0

### New
- **All WoW client languages**: English, German, French, Spanish (ES/MX), Italian, Portuguese (BR), Russian, Korean, Chinese (simplified/traditional) – always the language of the game client
- **Classic Era, WoW Forever and TBC Classic / Anniversary**: level 70, Outland dungeons, TBC attunements and dungeon/heroic keys, TBC portals and cooldowns, 25-player raid targets; on WoW Forever paladins and shamans on both factions plus the new dungeons and raids in the group finder
- **Overview** start page: guild health at a glance and a "for you" list
- **Leadership** (officers): probation tracker, mentor suggestion, departures with reason, officer notes with history, rank rights
- **My week**: lockouts per character, dailies, raid prep checklist
- **Board**: loot wishes per dungeon (shown in the group finder) and questions to the guild
- **Progress**: guild achievements, chronicle with Discord export, weekly recap in guild chat
- **Welcome guide** for new members
- Group finder shows the **zone** of each player
- **Features** can be turned on and off individually
- **Test mode** with a made-up guild, event buttons and "Click through everything"

### Fixed
- Error when showing prices on the board and market values for cooldowns
- Escape closes an open dropdown first; long dropdowns show all entries in columns
- Watched recipes take the profession from the item data; unknown ad placeholders are marked
- Layout fixes (member filters, export, attunement table, add-ons page, placeholders)
