AH Analyzer Pro

Market analysis, sniper and gold-making toolkit for the auction house – for every WoW version.

⚠️ This addon is in beta. It may still contain bugs. If you run into an error or unexpected behaviour in-game, please report it in the comments – ideally with the Lua error message (if any) and the WoW version you play on.

AH Analyzer Pro scans the auction house, keeps a price history for every item and turns it into market values, trends and charts. It finds bargains for you, helps you sell at the right price and keeps a ledger of everything you earn and spend. It runs as its own tab inside the auction house window, can be used completely with the mouse and speaks the language of your WoW client.

What's new

Version 0.59.33

Comfort update - twelve small things that save clicks every day:

  • Stack size and number of stacks (classic auction house): in the Sell tab, choose e.g. 5 stacks of 20. Remembered per item and used by "Post all".
  • Collect mail automatically: opening the mailbox collects auction mail right away (hold Shift while opening to skip). Can be turned off in Options.
  • Minimum price per item: the new "Min" button in the Sell tab remembers the current price as a floor. The suggestion never goes below it, and "Post all" waits instead of selling cheap.
  • Only what I'm missing: next to your profession window, a new button puts only the missing materials on a shopping list - counting your bags, bank and alts.
  • Alt-click an item in your bags or a link in chat: opens the price history, or the Buy tab while the auction house is open.
  • Sell rate in the tooltip: how much of what you posted actually sold (last 60 days) and how long it usually took.
  • Expiring auctions: a message when your auctions have less than 30 minutes left, and at login what expired since your last visit.
  • Favorites in the Buy tab: save searches (text, category, level, iLvl, rarity) and run them again with one click.
  • Gold goal: set a goal for all characters together (Options or /ahp goal 5000) and get a message when you reach it. The daily summary shows your progress.
  • Guild bank value: new view Ledger -> "Guild bank" shows what is in your guild bank and what it is worth (TBC and later).
  • Raid supplies: one click creates a shopping list with potions, flasks, elixirs and buff food for a raid week (Classic, TBC, Wrath) - with the total cost. Also /ahp raid.
  • Price sync with your guild only: new option to share prices only with your guild.

Version 0.59.32

  • No more absurd profits: items that would show a profit of 1000 % or more (almost always bad data such as an outlier market value or placeholder prices) are no longer listed in the Profit tab (all views: market value, vendor, disenchant, bids, flips) and no longer appear under "Biggest price increases" in the Overview.

Version 0.59.31

  • Item level as a search filter: the Buy tab and the Market tab now have "iLvl from" / "iLvl to" fields. They filter the results instantly, without a new search on the server, and also apply to the single listings of an item. In the Buy tab you can also search with only an iLvl range.

Version 0.59.30

  • Sell junk at vendors: at any vendor a button below the window sells grey items and everything a vendor pays more for than the auction house (after the AH cut). The tooltip lists what will be sold. Can be turned off in Options.
  • Inventory value: new view Ledger -> "Assets" shows what your bags, bank and alts are worth (market value and vendor value), plus the gold of all characters.
  • Daily summary at login: once a day the chat shows yesterday's profit, the last 7 days, the gold of all characters and how many items are waiting to be reposted. Any time with /ahp summary. Can be turned off in Options.
  • Repost with one click: expired or cancelled auctions that came back by mail are remembered. In the Auctions tab, "Repost (n)" posts them again one click at a time, undercutting the current price, with price protection.
  • Flip recommendations: new view Profit -> "Flips": items that are listed regularly and are currently well below market value. Stars show the turnover, sorted by expected profit.
  • Mail to alts: set rules like "Herbs -> Alchemist" (Options -> Mail to alts). At the mailbox the "To alts" button sends one mail per click with up to 12 matching stacks.
  • Buy tab: the iLvl column now shows the item level for every item (also for items the game had not loaded yet) and can be sorted.
  • Sell tab - listings appear faster: listings show up while they are still loading, a click on an item now goes before the bargain watcher and sniper, and hovering over an item already starts loading its listings.
  • Chat help: /ahp help (or /ahp ?) lists all commands in the chat. Unknown commands show the list too.

Version 0.59.29

  • Bid sniping: new view Profit -> "Bids" lists auctions whose next bid is far below market value, with the time left. They are collected during the full scan (classic auction house) and every search in the Buy tab. Click an item to open it in the Buy tab and bid. (Thanks paladriel for the idea!)
  • Price protection when posting: warns if your price is far below market value or follows a suspiciously cheap single unit - a second click is then needed, and "Post all" skips such items. Threshold in Options (off / 20 / 30 / 40 / 50 %, default 40 %).
  • Sale notification: as soon as one of your auctions sells you get a message with the proceeds after the AH cut and your profit compared to what you paid, plus a coin sound. Can be turned off in Options.

Older versions: see the changelog of each file in the Files tab.

Features

Market data

  • Full market scan with price history for every item
  • Market value = median of the last 14 days, so single outliers barely count
  • Trends and charts with time-based EMAs and crossover markers
  • Bait-offer protection and automatic re-checks of suspiciously cheap prices
  • Price sync between all players using the addon
  • Prices and a small price chart in every item tooltip, plus vendor price and disenchant value

Buying

  • Sniper: targets with max price, quantity, budget, rarity, item level and level filters – searches on its own while the auction house is open
  • Bargain watcher: herbs, ore, cloth, leather, enchanting, elemental, gems, cooking and more – anything at or below x % of market value (default 50 %); never weapons or armour
  • One-notch buying with the mouse wheel or your own key binding
  • Shopping lists with quantities – check all prices at once or turn missing items into sniper targets; items already in your bags are subtracted
  • Watchlist with alerts

Selling

  • Sell tab: price check and undercut suggestion (1 copper, 1 % or 5 %), deposit, AH cut and proceeds – warns when a vendor pays more or when you would sell below your purchase price
  • Series posting: posts every bag item with a remembered price, one click per auction
  • Auctions tab: status of your auctions (lowest / tied / undercut / sold), cancel with one click
  • Collect AH mail: gold and items from all auction mail with one click (COD is never paid)

Profit & bookkeeping

  • Ledger: income after the AH cut, spending, deposits and profit for today / 7 days / 30 days / all time – read from your mailbox invoices, nothing counted twice
  • Statistics: sell-through per item, best selling hours and weekdays
  • Profit tab: bargains below market value, below vendor price or below disenchant value; flipping calculator
  • Professions: material cost, value of the result and profit for every recipe, best recipes, materials straight to a shopping list
  • Vendor prices are learned automatically at every merchant
  • Alts & gold: gold, bags and bank of all your characters, gold history of the last 30 days
  • CSV export

Comfort

  • Two designs: Classic (looks like the WoW auction house) and Modern
  • Item search by name in any language, by item ID or by shift-click, with suggestions while typing
  • Built-in item name list (~23,000 items in 10 languages)
  • Help page for every tab, right-click menu on every item, minimap button

Supported game versions

AH Analyzer Pro works on every WoW version – all with the same features:

  • Retail
  • Mists of Pandaria Classic and Cataclysm Classic
  • Wrath Classic and Wrath Titan Reforged
  • TBC Anniversary
  • Classic Era (incl. Hardcore / Season realms)
  • WoW Forever

Both auction house types are supported: the modern one (Retail, Cata, MoP) and the classic one (Classic Era, TBC, Wrath).

One download for everything – just copy the folder AHAnalyzerPro into Interface\AddOns of your game.

Languages

English, German, French, Spanish (EU and Latin America), Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Korean, Chinese (simplified and traditional). The addon follows your client language automatically; you can change it in the options or with /ahp language.

Commands

Everything works with the mouse – the commands are optional.

Command Action
/ahp open / close the window
/ahp help list all commands in the chat
/ahp scan start a full market scan
/ahp sniper sniper on / off
/ahp summary daily summary in the chat
/ahp alts rules for mail to alts
/ahp goal 5000 gold goal (no number: progress, off: clear)
/ahp raid raid supplies as a shopping list
/ahp lists shopping lists
/ahp options options
/ahp sync price sync on / off
/ahp design switch between Classic and Modern
/ahp language change the addon language
/ahp export CSV export
/ahp donate support the addon (PayPal link)
/ahp manual help window with guide
/ahp reset reset window and button positions
