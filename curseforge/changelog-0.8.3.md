## GuildSentinel 0.8.3

### New
- **Support button**: a "Support" button at the top of the window (next to Close) and in Settings > General opens a small window with the PayPal link, ready to copy (Ctrl+C, then paste it into your browser). Also with `/gs donate` (or `/gs support`).
- It only opens when you click it or type the command – no pop-ups, no reminders, no ads in chat.
- Available in all client languages.
